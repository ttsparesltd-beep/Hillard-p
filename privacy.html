The information we collect

Account details

Your email address, which we use to sign you in securely via a one-time link (a "magic link"). We do not store a password.

Your contact details

Your name and mobile phone number, so we can identify you and contact you about your vehicle and bookings.

Vehicle information

Your vehicle registration, and details such as make, model, year, and MOT and service dates. Some of these details are retrieved automatically from the DVLA when you enter your registration.

Bookings and service records

Booking requests you make, descriptions of the work or fault, your service history with us, and any photographs of your vehicle that you choose to upload to support a booking.

Billing records

Invoices and related records for work carried out.

Technical information

Basic technical and log data needed to operate the app securely, such as access times and error logs.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To look up your vehicle details from your registration.
  • To take, manage and confirm your bookings.
  • To send you booking confirmations and MOT or service reminders.
  • To keep your service history and invoices.
  • To contact you about your vehicle, bookings or our services.
  • To maintain the security of, and improve, the app.

Our legal basis

We rely on the following lawful bases under the UK GDPR:

  • Contract — to provide the bookings, servicing and account features you ask for.
  • Legitimate interests — to run, secure and improve our service and to keep you informed about your vehicle (such as MOT reminders), in a way you would reasonably expect.
  • Consent — where we ask for it, for example any optional marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — to keep records we are required by law to keep, such as for tax.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the app, who process it on our behalf under contract:

  • Supabase — secure database, sign-in and hosting (data stored in the UK / London region).
  • Cloudinary — storage of vehicle photos you upload.
  • Resend — sending booking confirmation and reminder emails.
  • DVLA — we send your vehicle registration to retrieve official vehicle and MOT information.
  • Apple — distribution of the app (including TestFlight while in testing).

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

International transfers

Your core data is held within the UK. Some providers (such as those handling email or image storage) may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK-approved standard contractual clauses to protect your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, vehicle and service records for as long as you are a customer and for a reasonable period afterwards, and we keep invoicing records for as long as the law requires (generally six years for tax purposes). Photos and booking details are kept only as long as needed for the related work and our records.

How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit, access is restricted to authorised people and systems, and our database enforces row-level access controls so customers can only see their own records.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information erased in certain circumstances;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • ask for a copy of certain data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children

The app is intended for our customers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, let you know.
